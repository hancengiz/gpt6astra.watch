what happens

Your agent will

Ask you to confirm your country and then ask one explicit yes/no privacy question before installing anything · never trust an AI relay, VPN, or timezone as your country · pick the best watcher for your setup (Codex account ground-truth if available, otherwise the crowd-status watcher) · schedule it every 10 minutes — systemd on Linux, launchd on macOS, Task Scheduler on Windows · fire a desktop notification when Astra lands.

privacy

What leaves your machine

Fetching SKILL.md records request-network country, timestamp, and a salted one-way IP hash for private, unpublished funnel analytics. If you consent, the watcher also sends a random installation ID, heartbeat times, successful still-waiting confirmations, and its access/completion time. Those verified waiting confirmations join the website's waiting total and disappear after missed checks or when access arrives. The server stores identifiers only as salted one-way hashes — never an account name, hostname, or raw IP. Everything is open source: github.com/hancengiz/gpt6astra.watch
